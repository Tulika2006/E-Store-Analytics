# E-Store-Analytics
An interactive Power BI report designed to analyze sales performance, profitability trends, regional metrics, and customer payment behaviors for an e-commerce platform.
# Short Description / Purpose
The E-Store Analytics Dashboard provides a centralized visual summary of e-commerce operations, tracking key performance indicators across product lines and geographic regions. Built for retail analysts and business stakeholders, it highlights monthly profitability shifts, top revenue-generating states, and customer purchasing patterns to drive data-backed operational decisions.

# Tech Stack
 Power BI Desktop – Report design, interactive layout, and visual components.

 Power Query – Data cleaning, attribute transformation, and table structure shaping.

 DAX – Formulated key aggregations (Sum of Amount, Sum of Quantity, Sum of Profit).

 File Format – .pbip / .pbit for source control and .jpg for documentation previews.

# Data Source
Source: Internal E-Commerce Sales Records (Orders and Details tables).

Dataset Scope: Captures sales metrics (Amount), volume (Quantity), net margins (Profit), order timing (Quarter, Month), customer names, destination states, product categories/sub-categories, and payment modes.

# Features / Highlights
Business Problem: E-commerce teams lack unified visibility into which product lines drive profits versus volume, making seasonal margin dips and payment trends hard to evaluate.

Goal: Deliver a clean single-page dashboard tracking core performance KPIs, regional demand, and payment channel breakdown.

Key Visuals Walkthrough:

Top KPI Cards: Summarizes primary metrics—438K Revenue, 6K Quantity, and 37K Profit.

Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering by Quarter (Qtr 1–Qtr 4) and State.

Profit by Month: Combo chart tracking seasonal peaks (Jan, Nov) and loss months (May).

Quantity by Category & Payment Mode: Donut charts highlighting top categories (Clothing 63%, Electronics 21%) and preferred payment channels (COD 44%, UPI 21%).

Regional & Product Performance: Horizontal bar charts identifying top revenue states (Maharashtra, MP) and high-margin sub-categories (Printers, Bookcases, Sarees).

Business Impact: Optimizes inventory planning in top-performing regions, guides targeted marketing for high-margin products, and streamlines payment gateway preferences.
